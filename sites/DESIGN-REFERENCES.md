## Dove's original direction

Dove combines a cobalt hero, citrus highlights, warm paper surfaces, large tightly set type and restrained italic contrast. An original paper stack illustration connects the visual identity to the product. A three step walkthrough shares the page's actual state, followed by a screenshot of the working local app and an access request form.

The references informed editorial choices. No third party logos, customer claims, metrics, testimonials or page artwork were copied into Dove. The existing app screenshot contains fictional sample data. The site clearly distinguishes the illustrative workflow and local app from live invitation access.
