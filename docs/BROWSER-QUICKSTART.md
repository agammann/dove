# Your first Dove package

Use this fictional walkthrough in the [browser workspace](https://dove-paperwork.alx21.chatgpt.site/workspace/) or your [local build](../sites/README.md#local-development). No account, model download or API key is needed. Download the repository's [Agreement](../sites/examples/Agreement.txt), [Acceptance](../sites/examples/Acceptance.txt) and [Invoice](../sites/examples/Invoice.txt) text files, or find them in the release ZIP under `sites/examples/`.

1. Open **Settings**. Save **Fictional Studio** as the business name and **100 Example Lane. Payment due 30 days after the invoice date.** as the billing details.
2. Return to **Work**, select **New work item**, and enter **Fictional logo handoff**, **Example Customer**, **Example Contact**, `contact@example.invalid`, and **Completed the logo and style guide.** Confirm the fictional contact and create the item.
3. In **Documents**, add all three TXT files. Expand their source pages and compare the delivered work, acceptance, purchase order, amount, recipient and payment dates.
4. In **Review**, select **Add requirement**. Enter **Confirmed amount**, choose category **amount** and decision **satisfied**. Choose `Agreement.txt`, page 1, and paste the exact quote **The final billing amount is USD 2400.00, including all applicable taxes.** Explain that you checked the originals and save the decision. Add a second requirement for **Payment term**, category **custom**, quoting **Payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.** Record that October 7 plus 30 days is November 6. These two items illustrate the manual controls; a real checklist should include every relevant source requirement.
5. Open **Package**. Use a generated invoice, enter `2400.00`, invoice date `2026-10-07` and due date `2026-11-06`. Choose `Agreement.txt` as amount evidence and paste the same amount quote. Keep all supporting documents selected. Confirm your review and select **Assemble package for review**.
6. Download both the invoice PDF and package ZIP. Check the customer email, amount, dates and original attachments. Only then check the approval box and select **Approve package**. This approves that exact file version; it sends nothing.
7. In **Settings**, select **Export complete backup**. Keep the ZIP privately outside browser storage. Restore it in a separate browser profile to check recovery. Restoration replaces that profile's workspace and requires package approval again.

You can also choose device analysis or optional OpenAI analysis in **Documents**. Suggestions remain unapproved until you review them. GPT-5.4 is the hosted default for comparisons across sources. The lower-cost mini model can omit calculated dates; calculate payment dates yourself. Browser models can omit checklist items even in short documents. Review every original and add missing requirements manually.

If a control fails, read the message at the top of the workspace. An invented or altered quote cannot be saved. Packaging requires at least one reviewed requirement, billing settings and valid dates. Adding a source or changing a decision invalidates earlier package approval. See [storage and limits](HOSTED-WORKSPACE.md) before using real documents.
