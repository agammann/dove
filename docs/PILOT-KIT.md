# Invitation pilot: three businesses, one paperwork problem

This kit is a proposed research plan. No customers have been recruited, no interviews have occurred, and no savings or demand are established.

## Participant profile and interview guide

Recruit three businesses that repeatedly finish project/service work but cannot invoice promptly because a purchase order, acceptance evidence or approved deliverable is missing. A studio, consulting firm and IT provider are useful examples, but shared behavior matters more than industry.

Ask for a recent concrete case before showing Dove:

1. Walk through the last completed work item whose invoice was delayed. What exact document or approval was missing?
2. How did you know it was missing, who was allowed to supply it, and where was that authority recorded?
3. Show the manual steps and artifacts, using redacted examples if necessary. How many minutes did each active task take?
4. What happens when the amount, recipient or acceptance evidence conflicts?
5. What would you require before letting software send a request? Which actions must always wait for you?
6. Which documents are safe to include in a customer billing package, and which must remain internal?
7. How often does this happen? Who handles it, and what existing software must remain in place?
8. After trying Dove, what did you correct or distrust? What did you still do elsewhere?
9. Would you choose to use it on another work item? What would make continuation worth paying for?

Do not convert unattended waiting time into labor saved. Distinguish remembered estimates from observed timings.

## Onboarding checklist

- Verify release checklist and live integration test before inviting a real business.
- Confirm the participant and operator have authority to upload the selected data and contact the chosen recipients.
- Agree pilot duration (proposed: four weeks), support contact, retention/deletion date and measurement consent.
- Create a dedicated organization and single-use invitation. Never share sample accounts with live data.
- Begin with one completed work item and an existing invoice if practical.
- Upload readable source documents, inspect extracted requirements and correct false positives.
- Add customer contacts and separately record outreach/delivery authorization and acceptance authority.
- Confirm follow-up permission and reminder limits. Keep automation paused until the participant understands it.
- Preview every customer-facing attachment. Approve exact package, then separately authorize delivery.
- Explain that sent/accepted by provider does not mean accepted by customer or paid.
- Demonstrate pause, error recovery, export and deletion before the first live send.

## Feedback form

For each completed work item capture:

- Organization pseudonym / work reference / date.
- Documents uploaded and requirement types.
- Correctly identified missing items / missed items / false alarms.
- Corrections made and why; source quality problems.
- Active manual baseline minutes (observed or recalled, clearly labeled).
- Dove setup minutes, active review/correction minutes, exception handling minutes.
- Support minutes from the participant and the operator.
- Request/reply counts, delivery status, and any unwanted or duplicate messages.
- Would you use Dove again on the next comparable work item? Why?
- Trust, clarity and effort scores (1–5), with specific examples.
- Open-ended improvement request and unresolved concern.

## Pilot scorecard

| Metric | Calculation | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Net active handling time saved | Comparable baseline active minutes minus Dove setup/review/correction/exception minutes | Per work item and per business |
| Detection precision | Correct missing-item alerts / all missing-item alerts | Weekly; manually adjudicated |
| Detection recall | Correct missing-item alerts / all truly missing items found in audit | Weekly |
| False alarms | Non-missing items flagged / reviewed work items | Weekly |
| Repeat usage | Comparable work items voluntarily started after first completed item | Weekly |
| Support burden | Operator support minutes and recurring issue count | Weekly |
| Direct cost | Actual model tokens + email counts + storage/backups allocated to work | Weekly |
| Paid continuation | Explicit yes/no decision with proposed price and conditions | Pilot end |

Keep a row for each business, including cases that abandon the workflow. Report sample size, missing observations and uncertainty. Use actual provider billing records for cost validation; modeled costs are hypotheses.

## Pricing hypothesis

Test a simple workspace price, provisionally $29–$59 per month, with a defined included work-item/email allowance. This is an interview hypothesis, not a published offer, validated willingness to pay or a commitment. Keep a usage cap and quote heavier use separately until real costs and support needs are known. Subscription checkout is deferred.

Compare pricing to measured net handling effort and the participant's current process. Do not imply that administrative readiness guarantees earlier payment. Model hosting, provider usage, support and onboarding time separately.

## Expansion decision

Expand only if three businesses repeatedly use Dove, measured savings exceed setup and review effort, at least two explicitly agree to paid continuation, and operating/support costs appear sustainable. A negative or mixed outcome should lead to a narrower problem definition or another pilot, not invented demand. Recruitment never blocks finishing the application.
