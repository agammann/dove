## Live hosted first responses

Three actual visitor-key requests passed through the compiled Worker and rendered interface, without automatic retries or response mocks. GPT-5.4 read a matching three-document job and proposed received requirements for purchase order, acceptance, deliverables, amount, billing recipient and invoice/payment timing without inventing a conflict. Its separate TXT agreement plus three-page PDF invoice response correctly identified USD 2400 versus USD 2800 and the printed October 25 due date versus November 1 implied by October 2 plus 30 days. The timing conflict retained all three source references.

GPT-5.4 mini identified both conflicts and retained the three timing references, but omitted the calculated November 1 date from its explanation. That frozen quality criterion failed and the first response was retained. No repeat was used to replace it. GPT-5.4 remains the default; mini's model-choice guidance now asks visitors to calculate payment dates themselves.

All returned quotes matched their cited document/page, model statuses did not approve requirements, and analysis created no package. These bounded examples establish neither general accuracy nor complete requirement discovery. Hosted fixtures separately exercise request limits, scope/quote validation, sanitized errors and cancellation. Real provider cancellation/billing and other inputs were not remeasured for v1.
